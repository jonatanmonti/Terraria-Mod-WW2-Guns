WW2 Guns adds 42 firearms from World War II: 6 for each major nation.
[c/FFD700:Axis] - Germany, Italy, Japan
[c/87CEFA:Allies] - United States, United Kingdom, Soviet Union, France

Pistols, bolt-action rifles, semi-automatics, submachine guns, machine guns, a trench shotgun, a sniper rifle and an anti-tank rifle.
All guns use bullets. Craft them at an Anvil (or Mythril Anvil in Hardmode), buy sidearms from the Arms Dealer, or find rare drops in the Dungeon.

Agrega 42 armas de la Segunda Guerra Mundial (6 por país). Incluye traducción al español.
